- The source archive and private privacy-review records remain excluded from publication. This notice does not change the publication allowlist or redactions.
- Related-material previews in `assets/material-previews/` are limited to reviewed cover/title portions of one first scan per material. Their titles, author/instructor names and dates identify the held material. Document bodies, exercises, figures, contact details, original PDFs and full OCR remain private. The exported image hashes and dimensions are recorded in `material-preview-manifest.json`.
- These third-party cover/title images are excluded from MIT. Their display does not grant permission to republish the underlying material or imply that a named instructor endorses this site. Some records have bibliographic metadata only; a held scan does not by itself establish use in a particular course.
