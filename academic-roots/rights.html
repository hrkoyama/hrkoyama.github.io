調査結果の確かさ

AIの回答や入力データは自動的に検証済みにはなりません。本人申告・公開資料・未確認の推論を区別し、関係の根拠と出典を確認してください。

Your work stays under your control

The operator does not relicense visitor-entered, imported or exported lineage data under MIT. Inputs and AI responses are not sent to the operator; your lineage is saved in your browser. Use the tool’s delete control to remove locally saved work.

Using your own AI & sharing

When you paste a research request into your AI service, that service receives the request. Recipients of shared text, images, JSON and links receive the information they contain. Check the external service’s terms and choose what you share.

Evidence & uncertainty

AI responses and imported data do not become verified automatically. Distinguish self-reports, public-source evidence and unverified inferences, and check the evidence for each relationship.
