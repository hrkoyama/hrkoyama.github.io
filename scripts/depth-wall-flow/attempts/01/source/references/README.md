# Reference audit / 出典の確認

Ghia, Ghia & Shin (1982), JCP48(3),387–411, DOI10.1016/0021-9991(82)90058-4 is the original primary research article. Publisher metadata/abstract was read; publisher PDF returned403. The original25-page paper was then read from the public mirror recorded in source-review.json. Relevant PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected: printed391–392 (equations3.1–3.5 and Fig1),398–399 (TablesI/II),408 (Re100 primary vortex in TableV). No statement that all25pages were audited is made. The mirror is a delivery location for the original paper, not a separate numerical authority.

`ghia-re100.csv` manually transcribes only the Re100 columns,17points each, of TablesI/II. Coordinates are the printed four-decimal values. We linearly interpolate our computed centerlines to exactly those printed coordinates; table rounding is not a discretization-error-free continuum reference. TableV gives the grid-based vortex center(.6172,.7344) and psi_min−.103423; only the center has an acceptance gate. The source PDF SHA256 is recorded but the third-party PDF and rendered pages are not redistributed.

Original paper equations define u=psi_y,v=−psi_x,omega=−Delta psi; Fig1 has a rightward unit top lid and other stationary walls. This code independently uses centered finite differences, Heun and Dirichlet DST Poisson, not Ghia's CSI-MG solver. Ghia's higher-order wall expression3.4–3.5 is not claimed as our closure. Our local Thom closure is derived by expanding psi at the wall: psi(h)=psi(0)+h*d_n psi(0)+h²*psi_nn(0)/2+O(h³). At a stationary flat no-slip wall omega_wall=−2psi_adj/h²+O(h); at the top moving wall the additional term is−2U/h. Velocity at the top corners is explicitly zero; corner vorticity is the mean of neighboring wall values and does not enter a five-point interior stencil.

Salari & Knupp's primary Sandia report SAND2000-1444, DOI10.2172/759450, publisher/OSTI abstract and UNT-hosted printed12 were read. That selected section motivates manufactured solutions together with mesh refinement; our polynomial is constructed here and is not transcribed from the report. Both flow cases use the same numerical wall closure. No exact manufactured wall vorticity is injected.

Independent qualification differentiates a tensor polynomial coefficient matrix with numpy.polynomial, independently of wall_flow.py's explicit formulas. It checks analytic fields/forcing/no-slip/divergence at fixed interior and boundary locations. This is independent algebra, not an independent Navier–Stokes solver. The manufactured pressure field separately checks the nonzero Dirichlet Poisson/gradient operator. Pressure is eliminated from the streamfunction evolution: no cavity pressure, pressure gauge, pressure boundary traction, or MAC projection is computed or validated.
