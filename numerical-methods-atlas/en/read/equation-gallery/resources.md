## Exact / reference solutions

- [Burgers shock — references and comparison conditions](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/problems/burgers-shock/?lang=en#references-en>)

  Compare with the analytic entropy shock for states 1 and 0 separated at x=0.5. Distinguish point values from conserved cell averages in finite-volume errors.
- [Burgers / shock](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/solutions/?lang=en#solution-burgers-shock-en>)
  relation: related-model
  reference_kind: analytic

  Analytic reference Entropy solution of a scalar whole-line Riemann problem; constant endpoint continuation. States (1,0), jump position 0.5 and endpoint continuation match; distinguish point and cell-average evaluation. Physical inputs match, but the card’s point samples and the saved computation’s finite-volume cell averages/discrete grid are different representations.
- [Burgers rarefaction — references and comparison conditions](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/problems/burgers-fan/?lang=en#references-en>)

  The standard is the analytic entropy rarefaction for states 0 and 1 separated at x=0.5.
- [Burgers / rarefaction](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/solutions/?lang=en#solution-burgers-fan-en>)
  relation: related-model
  reference_kind: analytic

  Analytic reference Entropy solution of a scalar whole-line Riemann problem; constant endpoint continuation. States (0,1), jump position 0.5 and endpoint continuation match. Physical inputs match, but the card’s point samples and the saved computation’s finite-volume cell averages/discrete grid are different representations.
- [Traffic jam shock — references and comparison conditions](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/problems/traffic-jam/?lang=en#references-en>)

  Analytically evaluate the entropy shock for Riemann densities 0.3 and 0.9. Distinguish vehicle motion from the shock speed.
- [Traffic / upstream shock](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/solutions/?lang=en#solution-traffic-jam-en>)
  relation: related-model
  reference_kind: analytic

  Analytic reference Entropy solution of a scalar whole-line Riemann problem; constant endpoint continuation. Flux ρ(1-ρ), left/right densities (0.3,0.9) and jump position 0.5 match. Physical inputs match, but the card’s point samples and the saved computation’s finite-volume cell averages/discrete grid are different representations.
- [Traffic clearing fan — references and comparison conditions](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/problems/traffic-fan/?lang=en#references-en>)

  The standard is the analytic entropy rarefaction for Riemann densities 0.9 and 0.1.
