Euler reconstruction: PPM, characteristics, WENO-Z and AUSM

What is compared#

The reconstruction view compares conservative-component and characteristic MUSCL/MC, limited parabolic finite volumes, and finite-difference WENO5-JS/Z. FV uses HLLC; FD uses global Lax–Friedrichs flux splitting. Both use SSPRK3, nominal CFL 0.3 and the same number of grid locations. Their representations, fluxes and error norms differ: this is not an isolated reconstruction or matched-cost ranking. Within each method, changing only the basis is a controlled component/characteristic comparison.

The separate flux view fixes conservative characteristic MUSCL/MC and SSPRK3, then switches HLLC, acoustic-smoothed Roe, Einfeldt-type HLLE and the explicitly defined AUSM baseline below. It does not change the spatial reconstruction.

Limited parabolic finite volumes#

Following the reconstruction idea of Colella–Woodward (1984), form a parabola in each cell from its mean and two edges. The inspected public 1982 author preprint is an earlier version, not the final journal layout. This implementation includes limited edge interpolation and monotonicity constraints, coupled by method of lines to SSPRK3. It excludes original PPM wave tracing, contact steepening, shock flattening, Lagrangian evolution and remap. Direct-Eulerian tracing, contact steepening and flattening are now available in the second computed stage; Lagrangian evolution and remap remain future work.

For a cell mean Q and edges L,R, use the local coordinate ξ in [0,1]:

q(ξ)=L+ξ[R−L+q6(1−ξ)],q6=6Q−3(L+R). q(\xi)=L+\xi[R-L+q_6(1-\xi)],\qquad q_6=6Q-3(L+R). q(ξ)=L+ξ[R−L+q6​(1−ξ)],q6​=6Q−3(L+R).

The integral is exactly Q. Limit initial edge interpolation between adjacent means; flatten a parabolic extremum and apply the usual curvature bounds. Characteristic projection freezes a local conservative eigenbasis in each cell, limits projected deviations, then transforms back. Components are conservative variables, not primitive variables. Reconstructed left/mid/right states share one affine positivity scaling about Q if needed. Their Simpson mean remains Q; no conserved mean is clipped. Inadmissible RK stages retry with a halved time step. These safeguards do not prove fully discrete positivity.

Characteristic finite-difference WENO-Z#

For each face freeze a Roe-averaged conservative eigenbasis. Project the positive and negative global-LF split flux stencils, reconstruct each characteristic component, and transform the resulting face flux back. FD evolves point values; it is not FV state reconstruction. Shared flux differences conserve the discrete sum on a periodic grid.

The Z variant uses the JS smoothness indicators with

τ5=∣β0−β2∣,αkZ=dk[1+(τ5βk+ϵ)2],ωkZ=αkZ∑lαlZ,(d0,d1,d2)=(0.1,0.6,0.3). \tau_5=|\beta_0-\beta_2|,\quad \alpha_k^Z=d_k\left[1+\left(\frac{\tau_5}{\beta_k+\epsilon}\right)^2\right], \quad\omega_k^Z=\frac{\alpha_k^Z}{\sum_l\alpha_l^Z}, \quad(d_0,d_1,d_2)=(0.1,0.6,0.3). τ5​=∣β0​−β2​∣,αkZ​=dk​[1+(βk​+ϵτ5​​)2],ωkZ​=∑l​αlZ​αkZ​​,(d0​,d1​,d2​)=(0.1,0.6,0.3).

Here ε = 10⁻⁶ is fixed and the Z exponent is 2. These choices must accompany comparisons; the regularization and normalization of the characteristics affect the weights. Borges, Carmona, Costa and Don (2008) is the original bibliographic reference. Publisher metadata and the public earlier three-author manuscript were inspected; that manuscript has different weight conventions. The formula above, its exponent and the executable checks define this edition's variant; no final-paper figure is reproduced.

AUSM: keep the variant explicit#

Liou–Steffen (1993) introduced AUSM. The local baseline uses a common interface sound speed a = (aL+aR)/2, Mach polynomials M± = ±(M±1)²/4 in the subsonic interval, and the cubic pressure fractions P± = (M±1)²(2∓M)/4. Supersonic branches use signed upwind Mach and pressure fractions. These formulas were cross-checked against the official FEST-3D AUSM source. This is an independently implemented, specified AUSM-family variant, not FEST-3D output or a reproduction of every 1993 sound-speed/pressure option.

Let Mf = M+(uL/a)+M−(uR/a). Upwind the advected vector (ρ,ρu,ρv,ρH) using a max(Mf,0) on the left and a min(Mf,0) on the right, and add pL P+(uL/a)+pR P−(uR/a) to normal momentum only. Energy transports total enthalpy. Tests include uniform states, both supersonic directions, Mach ±1 branch limits, stationary/moving contacts and the computed Euler problems.

AUSM+ (1996) and AUSM+-up (2006) are separately defined in the new traced-PPM comparison. The current baseline has no all-speed correction or low-Mach verification. None of these names implies universal shock stability; inspect the separate odd–even comparison.

Problems, references and accuracy#

  • Sod: N=200, x∈[0,1], t=0.2, standard states, extrapolated ends.
  • Moving contact: N=200, periodic [0,1], u=p=1, density 1 on the first half and 2 on the second; one crossing t=1.
  • Shu–Osher: N=240, x∈[−5,5], t=1.8, fixed end states, shock at −4, left (ρ,u,p)=(3.857143,2.629369,10.33333), right density 1+0.2sin(5x), u=0,p=1. The dashed curve is our internal characteristic FD WENO-Z N=960 run, not an exact solution or an external-code validation.

FV initialization integrates conserved quantities. Sod final density errors use 128-node Gauss quadrature of the analytic Riemann solution; the saved 64/128-node discrepancy estimates quadrature sensitivity across discontinuities. FD errors are pointwise. Derived FV primitive curves are primitive variables of conserved means, not exact primitive means. No exact Shu–Osher error is reported.

A separate smooth periodic entropy wave uses N=32,64,128 at t=0.2 and CFL = 0.25(32/N)^(2/3). This reduces dt proportional to dx^(5/3), making the SSPRK3 time error O(dx⁵). Measured WENO orders are about five on this smooth case; limited PPM is about two on this sequence because of extremum limiting. This is not a global fifth-order guarantee at shocks or arbitrary critical points.

Reproduce with python scripts/build_reconstruction.py. Download profiles, diagnostics and source hashes and conserved arrays. The final arrays, positive-state checks and retry counts apply only to the stated tests.
