## Exact / reference solutions

- [One-dimensional strong pressure jump — references and comparison conditions](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/problems/strong-shock/?lang=en#references-en>)

  A semi-analytic Riemann reference is stored for this strong pressure jump. Its initial states differ from the library's standard Sod problem.
- [Cylinder-forebody bow shock — references and comparison conditions](<https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/problems/cylinder-forebody/?lang=en#references-en>)

  Reference material consists of stored body-fitted fields, wall pressure and histories for two grids with and without perturbations. It certifies neither a steady state nor elimination of carbuncles.
