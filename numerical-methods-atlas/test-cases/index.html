  • 補助例csvr-kinetic-kernelはCSVR熱浴単独の条件付き分布・揺らぎ・相関の診断として別掲し、独立した物理テスト問題へは加算しません。調和系のCSVR/Langevin比較も既存langevin-harmonicへの手法追加として統合し、四次ポテンシャルのthermostatted-quarticだけを新しい問題として1件加算します。
  • mcmc-diagnostic-referenceはACF・rank Rhat・bulk/tail ESSと退化条件の診断対照で、物理問題数へ加算しません。2峰混合の初期値・局所/反射提案・seed違いはmcmc-bimodal-mixtureの1問題へ統合し、旧単峰gaussian-mcmcとは別に数えます。混合不良を期待通り再現した検査の合格は、そのシナリオの推定成功を意味しません。

  • The glm-fourier-mode supplement is a longitudinal GLM divergence-error damping diagnostic and does not add an independent physical test problem.
  • The csvr-kinetic-kernel supplement diagnoses conditional laws, fluctuations and correlations of the isolated CSVR bath and adds no physical problem. The harmonic CSVR/Langevin comparison extends the existing langevin-harmonic problem; only the quartic-potential thermostatted-quartic case adds one new problem.
  • mcmc-diagnostic-reference is an ACF/rank-Rhat/bulk-tail-ESS and degeneracy control, not an additional physical problem. Different starts, local/reflection proposals and seeds for the bimodal target form one mcmc-bimodal-mixture problem, distinct from the old unimodal gaussian-mcmc. Passing an intentional mixing-failure check does not mean estimation succeeded in that scenario.
  • amg-conditioning is a known-algebraic-solution diagnostic of conditioning, residuals and anisotropy, with no physical-problem binding; its evidence is not borrowed for Poisson mesh accuracy. QR/SVD, Krylov and quadrature conditioning families extend matrix-factorization, krylov-spectrum and quadrature-moments respectively; parameters and diagnostic controls do not add separate problems.
