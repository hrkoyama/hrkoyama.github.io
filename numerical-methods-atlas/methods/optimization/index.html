この例の前提

  • 固定格子・固定Dirichlet境界の滑らかなBratu下側分枝を用い、状態ヤコビ行列が局所的に可逆である。
  • 固定した内部5点の観測、既知の正の観測重み、区間制約を持つ1個のスカラーパラメータλを用いる。
  • 射影勾配法とArmijo線探索の探索方向に随伴勾配を用い、収束判定と生成パラメータの既知値との比較を分ける。

境界条件: u(0)=u(1)=0をλに依存せず固定し、観測位置も区間[0,1]内で固定する。

単位系: 状態とλは無次元。重み付き最小二乗の観測重みを明記し、空間積分の求積重みと区別する。

Assumptions for this example

  • Smooth, locally nonsingular lower-branch Bratu states on a fixed mesh; fixed Dirichlet data.
  • Five fixed interior sensors, known positive observation weights and one bounded scalar parameter lambda.
  • Projected-gradient Armijo iteration uses adjoint gradients; convergence is checked independently of the known generating parameter.

Boundaries: u(0)=u(1)=0, independent of lambda; observation locations fixed on [0,1].

Units: Dimensionless state and lambda; weighted least squares uses explicit sensor weights, not spatial integration weights.

Bratu inverse problem: adjoint-gradient parameter fitting

An educational one-parameter inverse problem on the smooth lower branch with fixed synthetic observations, bounded projected gradients and Armijo search. Same-discrete-model recovery, grid bias against continuous analytic observations and shifts under fixed noise are distinguished. Quasi-Newton methods, real data, branch identification and statistical confidence intervals are outside the verified scope.
