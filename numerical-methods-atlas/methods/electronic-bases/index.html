比較するときの注意

基底を変える操作と電子相関・汎関数を変える操作は別です。線形従属、基底重なり誤差、擬ポテンシャルとの整合性も確認します。

この例の前提

  • Born–Oppenheimer・非相対論・孤立H₂、2電子の閉殻RHF。
  • STO-3Gと入れ子の原子中心s型Gaussian基底を比較。角運動量を増やす分極関数は含まない。
  • 重なり行列の固有値・条件数を確認。線形従属に近い入力は基底を黙って削らず失敗させる。

境界条件: 孤立した有限分子。空間全域の解析Gaussian積分で、有限箱の境界は設けない。

単位系: 原子単位系：距離bohr、エネルギーhartree。Gaussian基底は空間全域で規格化。

H₂の有限Gaussian基底RHF

新しいH₂の閉殻RHF例：自作s型Gaussian積分、独立PySCF比較、SCF残差・基底拡大を確認。有限のs型部分空間に限定し、一般分子・分子DFT・周期系・正しい解離極限は検証しません。

適用範囲と残る作業

Comparison notes

Changing the basis is different from changing electron correlation or the functional. Check near-linear dependence, basis-set superposition error and pseudopotential compatibility.

Assumptions for this example

  • Born–Oppenheimer, nonrelativistic isolated H2 closed-shell RHF, two electrons.
  • STO-3G comparison and nested atom-centered s-type Gaussian spaces; angular polarization is not included.
  • Overlap eigenvalues and condition numbers are checked; nearly dependent inputs fail instead of silently deleting functions.

Boundaries: Isolated finite molecule; analytic whole-space Gaussian integrals with no finite spatial box.

Units: Atomic units in the displayed electronic equations (hbar = m_e = e = 4 pi epsilon_0 = 1); dimensionless conventions are stated where needed.

H₂ RHF in finite Gaussian bases

New H₂ closed-shell RHF example: original s-Gaussian integrals, independent PySCF comparisons, SCF residuals and basis refinement. Limited to finite s-type subspaces; does not validate general molecules, molecular DFT, periodic systems or correct dissociation.

Independent reference, SCF and basis refinement · Python solver · Tests · ZIP

Shared input, execution, validation and citation steps

Scope and remaining work

Basis refinement in an H₂ s-Gaussian subspace. Monotone energy lowering alone is not convergence: report the last change and stated tolerance. No complete-basis, angular-momentum or systematic BSSE validation.
