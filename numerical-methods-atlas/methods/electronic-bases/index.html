Comparison notes

Changing the basis is different from changing electron correlation or the functional. Check near-linear dependence, basis-set superposition error and pseudopotential compatibility.

Assumptions for this example

  • Atomic/molecular examples: Born–Oppenheimer, nonrelativistic isolated H2 closed-shell RHF, two electrons.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: STO-3G comparison and nested atom-centered s-type Gaussian spaces; angular polarization is not included.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: Overlap eigenvalues and condition numbers are checked; nearly dependent inputs fail instead of silently deleting functions.
  • Periodic KS-DFT for one fixed simple-cubic He cell. An educational PySCF workflow separately checks Gamma/k sampling, Gaussian orbital basis, auxiliary FFT grid and SCF tolerances against independent DFTK plane waves with identical GTH parameters and LDA exchange plus VWN5 correlation. Does not validate equilibrium helium solids, general materials or metals.

Boundaries: Atomic/molecular examples: Isolated finite molecule; analytic whole-space Gaussian integrals with no finite spatial box. Periodic He: three-dimensional Bloch boundaries in the fixed one-atom simple-cubic cell.

Units: Atomic/molecular examples: Atomic units in the displayed electronic equations (hbar = m_e = e = 4 pi epsilon_0 = 1); dimensionless conventions are stated where needed. Periodic He: bohr, inverse bohr and hartree per neutral primitive cell; reciprocal-lattice fractional k coordinates.

H₂ RHF in finite Gaussian bases

New H₂ closed-shell RHF example: original s-Gaussian integrals, independent PySCF comparisons, SCF residuals and basis refinement. Limited to finite s-type subspaces; does not validate general molecules, molecular DFT, periodic systems or correct dissociation.

Periodic He₂ forces and the BOMD interface

Asymmetric two-He periodic cell with local GTH and LDA exchange plus VWN5. Compare PySCF analytic forces, including Gaussian-basis derivatives, with energy finite differences for all six coordinates and independent DFTK plane waves. Separate force basis, k-point, SCF and FFT refinement. BOMD coverage is a force interface and short step comparison; long NVE, CPMD and general materials remain unvalidated.

Scope and remaining work

Atomic/molecular example scope: Basis refinement in an H₂ s-Gaussian subspace. Monotone energy lowering alone is not convergence: report the last change and stated tolerance. No complete-basis, angular-momentum or systematic BSSE validation. A separate PySCF periodic-He driver additionally uses VWN5 correlation and periodic k-point quadrature.
