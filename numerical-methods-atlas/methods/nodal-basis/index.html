解く問題と採用形

解く問題
節点値からの多項式補間、微分と参照要素行列。
採用した変種・範囲
Legendre Vandermondeで構成する区間Lagrange基底、GLL/等間隔節点、明示P1/P2三角形、Qₚ積基底。

使える条件

  • 節点自由度、partition of unity、モード表現との同値性を調べるとき。
  • GLLで対角化した求積質量と厳密質量の違いを比較するとき。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 任意次数の三角形・曲線要素や大規模高次数表作成を求める場合。
  • GLLの対角質量を常に厳密だとみなす場合。

解説と位置付け

解説章を読む

前提・比較仕様

  • 区間節点は相異なる有限実数で、中程度の次数を想定する。区間のLagrange多項式はLegendre Vandermonde行列を解いて評価する。
  • p≥1のGLL節点は両端±1とPₚ′の根。p+1点GLL求積は2p−1次まで厳密で、次数2pの質量積分は一般には厳密でない。
  • 三角形は (0,0),(1,0),(0,1) のP1またはP2のみ。四角形は区間基底のQₚ積で、自由度数は(p+1)²。

条件を記載: 節点条件、GLL次数、三角形/積基底の範囲を確認した。

境界条件: GLLは端点を含むが、それだけでDirichlet値や要素間の連続性を課すわけではない。境界値・流束・接続は利用側が指定する。

この構成要素では対象外: 端点節点の存在と物理境界の施行を区別した。

単位系: 参照座標と基底は無次元、節点値は表す関数の単位。アフィン区間行列はJ=L/2により M∝J、K∝1/J。

条件を記載: 参照座標とアフィン要素の尺度を明示した。

Method-specific conditions and sources: 2026-11-22

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Polynomial interpolation/differentiation from nodal values and reference-element matrices.
Variant and scope
Interval Lagrange bases via a Legendre Vandermonde solve, GLL/equispaced nodes, explicit triangle P1/P2 and Qₚ products.

Use when

  • Studying nodal degrees of freedom, partition of unity and modal equivalence.
  • Comparing quadrature-diagonalized GLL mass with exact mass.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Arbitrary-degree triangles, curved elements or large high-degree tabulation.
  • Treating GLL diagonal quadrature mass as universally exact.

Read the formulation

Read the chapter

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • Interval nodes must be distinct finite real values at modest degree; cardinal polynomials are evaluated through a Legendre Vandermonde solve.
  • For p≥1, GLL nodes are ±1 and roots of Pₚ′. A p+1-point GLL rule is exact through degree 2p−1, not generally for degree-2p mass products.
  • Triangle functions are only P1/P2 on (0,0),(1,0),(0,1). Quadrilaterals use Qₚ tensor products with (p+1)² degrees of freedom.

Specified: Checked node conditions, GLL degree and triangle/tensor-product scope.

Boundaries: GLL includes endpoints but does not itself enforce Dirichlet values or interelement continuity. The consuming discretization supplies values, fluxes and coupling.

Not applicable to this component: Distinguished endpoint nodes from physical boundary enforcement.

Units: Reference coordinates/basis values are dimensionless; nodal values carry the function's units. Affine interval matrices scale as M∝J and K∝1/J with J=L/2.

Specified: Specified reference coordinates and affine-element scaling.

Shared input, execution, validation and citation steps

Scope and remaining work

High-degree Vandermonde solves face conditioning limits and are not a replacement for stable barycentric tabulation or a general high-order element library. High-degree equispaced interpolation can exhibit Runge behavior. The rank-one exact/lumped GLL mass difference concerns the unweighted affine interval, not arbitrary triangles.

Specified: Scoped quadrature accuracy, conditioning and element families.
