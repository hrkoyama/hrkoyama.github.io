適用範囲と残る作業

Gaussian標的のMCMC：分布・自己相関・ESS: 固定seed・予算での統計検査です。ESSは平均という観測量だけ、Rhatはrank正規化版ではありません。多峰性・高次元・任意のseedでの混合や所定の偽陽性率は保証しません。 多峰性MCMC：峰への閉じ込め・偽の安心・大域提案: 固定seed・有限予算の教育用実験です。局所Gaussian RWも理論上は既約なMHであり、有限時間のtrappingを非エルゴードなアルゴリズムとは呼びません。小さいRhat・大きいESS・プールCDFの一致は未発見の峰を除外せず、既知対称性のreflection提案も一般多峰ソルバーではありません。量子計算は追加しません。 MCMC診断の独立照合：rank Rhat・bulk/tail ESS・退化条件: 固定seed・有限予算の教育用実験です。局所Gaussian RWも理論上は既約なMHであり、有限時間のtrappingを非エルゴードなアルゴリズムとは呼びません。小さいRhat・大きいESS・プールCDFの一致は未発見の峰を除外せず、既知対称性のreflection提案も一般多峰ソルバーではありません。量子計算は追加しません。 定数observableのESS=NというArviZ raw規約を混合の証拠にしません。全同値はnull＋status、鎖ごとに異なる定数のRhatはnull＋infinite-separationとして、ライブラリの巨大finite/infを残し通常有限値の一致点から除外します。bulk ESSは原スケール平均のMCSEへ流用しません。 今回の追加固定例：診断engineの独立性とsamplerの独立参照を混同しません。Rhat等は大域収束・被覆の証明ではありません。isotropicは固定比較で失敗を要求せず、banana/funnel/HMC/NUTSは未実装です。

Educational example / related code

Gaussian-target MCMC: distribution, autocorrelation and ESS: Check 32 Metropolis chains against a known standard-normal target; record mean, variance, CDF, autocorrelation time, ESS and sample-budget dependence. Multimodal MCMC: trapping, false reassurance and a global proposal: Compare dispersed local MH, same-mode local MH and symmetry-informed reflection/local MH on the same bimodal target. Separate detecting expected mixing failures from successful finite-budget mode exploration. Independent MCMC diagnostic checks: rank Rhat, bulk/tail ESS and degeneracies: Actually process62 byte-identical datasets with separately installed ArviZ0.20.0, bound by recipe/input/chain/official-source hashes. Separate finite-value agreement from explicit degeneracy-policy differences.

Scope and remaining work

Gaussian-target MCMC: distribution, autocorrelation and ESS: Statistical checks apply to fixed seeds and budgets. ESS concerns the mean only; Rhat is not rank-normalized. No guarantee covers multimodality, high dimensions, every seed, mixing or a specified false-positive rate. Multimodal MCMC: trapping, false reassurance and a global proposal: An educational fixed-seed, finite-budget experiment. The local Gaussian RW is theoretically irreducible MH; finite-time trapping is not called a nonergodic algorithm. Small Rhat, large ESS and pooled-CDF agreement cannot rule out undiscovered modes. Symmetry-informed reflection is not a generic multimodal solver. No quantum calculations. Independent MCMC diagnostic checks: rank Rhat, bulk/tail ESS and degeneracies: An educational fixed-seed, finite-budget experiment. The local Gaussian RW is theoretically irreducible MH; finite-time trapping is not called a nonergodic algorithm. Small Rhat, large ESS and pooled-CDF agreement cannot rule out undiscovered modes. Symmetry-informed reflection is not a generic multimodal solver. No quantum calculations. ArviZ raw constant-ESS=N is not evidence of mixing. Globally identical values yield null/status; differing per-chain constants yield null/infinite-separation Rhat while retaining raw huge-finite/infinite library values outside ordinary finite comparisons. Bulk ESS is not a raw-mean MCSE estimator. Additional fixed examples: Diagnostic-engine independence is not an independent sampler. Rhat/ESS/MCSE do not prove global convergence/coverage. Isotropic is descriptive with no required failure;banana/funnel/HMC/NUTSremain unimplemented.
