解く問題と採用形

解く問題
滑らかな常微分方程式・空間離散後の系の初期値問題。
採用した変種・範囲
runge_kutta.TABLEAUS['rk4']の古典4段4次とrk_step。Hamiltonian比較にも同じ古典係数のRK4がある。

使える条件

  • 非硬い滑らかな問題で、誤差と刻み幅の関係を明快に調べたい。
  • 4回の右辺評価を許容でき、固定刻みや別の刻み制御を管理できる。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 非常に硬い減衰やDAEを汎用に解く。
  • SSP性・正値性や長時間のHamilton構造保存が必須。

解説と位置付け

解説章を読む

前提・比較仕様

  • 対象は初期値問題y'=f(t,y)で、4次収束を期待する区間では右辺と解に十分な滑らかさがある。
  • 採用係数はc=(0,1/2,1/2,1)、a21=1/2、a32=1/2、a43=1、b=(1,2,2,1)/6の古典4段4次。
  • 時間刻みは呼び出し側が選び、空間離散したPDEではそのスペクトル・CFL制約にも従う。線形モードでは|R(hλ)|≤1を確認する。

条件を記載: 公開tableauと非自律段階時刻を確認。

境界条件: 時間ステップ単体に空間境界条件は該当しない。PDEを進める場合、境界値やゴースト状態は各段階の右辺評価が正しく供給する必要がある。

この構成要素では対象外: rk_stepは右辺関数を受け取る時間積分部品。

単位系: 時間hとtは同じ単位、f_iはy_i/時間の単位。状態成分の単位は問題が定め、RK4自体は固有の物理単位を持たない。hλは無次元。

条件を記載: y+h fという段階式から成分ごとに定義。

適用範囲と残る作業

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Initial-value problems for smooth ODEs or semidiscrete systems.
Variant and scope
Classical four-stage fourth-order TABLEAUS['rk4'] and rk_step in runge_kutta.py; the Hamiltonian comparison also uses classical RK4 coefficients.

Use when

  • A smooth nonstiff problem needs a clear accuracy/step-size baseline.
  • Four RHS evaluations per step are affordable, with fixed-step or external step control.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Very stiff decay or general DAEs must be handled.
  • SSP/positivity guarantees or long-time Hamiltonian structure preservation are essential.

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • The target is the initial-value problem y'=f(t,y), with sufficient smoothness of f and y for fourth-order convergence.
  • The adopted classical tableau has c=(0,1/2,1/2,1), a21=1/2, a32=1/2, a43=1 and b=(1,2,2,1)/6.
  • The caller selects the step. A semidiscrete PDE also imposes spatial spectral/CFL restrictions; linear modes require |R(hλ)|≤1.

Specified: Inspected the released tableau and nonautonomous stage times.

Boundaries: Spatial boundary conditions do not apply to the ODE step itself. For a PDE, each stage's RHS evaluation must supply the appropriate boundary values or ghost states.

Not applicable to this component: rk_step is a time integrator that receives an RHS function.

Units: h and t have the same time unit, and f_i has units y_i/time. State-component units belong to the problem; RK4 imposes no intrinsic physical unit. hλ is dimensionless.

Specified: Specified componentwise from stage updates of the form y+h f.

Scope and remaining work

Explicit RK4 is neither A-stable nor L-stable and may require small steps for stiff decay. It has no positive general nonlinear SSP coefficient; fourth-order accuracy implies neither positivity, TVD, energy conservation nor symplecticity. The released rk_step is a fixed-step component without an embedded error estimator or adaptive controller.

Specified: Separates linear stability from nonlinear SSP and geometric properties.

Scope of this review

Read the RK4 tableau, rk_step and stability functions in runge_kutta.py. Inspected tests for fourth-order conditions, a manufactured nonautonomous nonlinear solution, stability functions, failure of general SSP conditions, and the Hamiltonian comparison. No rerun was performed.
