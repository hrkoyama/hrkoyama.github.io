解く問題と採用形

解く問題
周期像を含む中性点電荷のCoulombエネルギーと力。孤立重力や電子密度のHartree場とは区別します。
採用した変種・範囲
公開例は中性3D点電荷・立方体・tin-foil境界の直接Ewald和です。実空間・逆空間・自己項を含み、打切り・分割パラメータ、エネルギー有限差分の力と独立theta積分を検査しています。

使える条件

  • 周期像を含むCoulombエネルギーと力を、明示した境界規約で評価したい場合。
  • 小さな中性系で、PME/SPMEと比較する打切り収束済みの基準を用意する場合。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 孤立・2次元周期・slab、または中和背景を指定していない非中性系。
  • 有限打切りの値を収束済みの厳密値と扱う場合。実空間と逆空間の誤差を別々に調べる必要があります。

前提・比較仕様

  • 最初の検証仕様は、立方体セル内の重なりのない点電荷と3次元周期Coulomb相互作用です。総電荷はゼロとし、一様中和背景は含めません。
  • 同一粒子の同一セル項（i=j, n=0）のみを除外し、自己の周期像との相互作用は残します。自己エネルギー補正を別に扱います。
  • 分割係数・実空間打切り・逆空間または格子条件を明示します。静電ポテンシャルの平均をゼロにする選択と、巨視的な表面項の境界規約は別です。

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Coulomb energy and forces for neutral point charges and periodic images, distinct from isolated gravity or an electron-density Hartree field.
Variant and scope
The released example is direct Ewald summation for neutral 3D point charges in a cubic cell with tin-foil boundaries. It includes real/reciprocal/self terms and checks cutoffs, splitting, energy-difference forces and an independent theta integral.

Use when

  • A periodically replicated Coulomb problem with explicitly stated boundary conventions.
  • A small neutral reference for same-convention PME/SPME energy and force comparisons.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Isolated, two-dimensionally periodic or slab systems, or nonneutral cells without an explicit background model.
  • Treating finite cutoffs as an exact reference without separate real- and reciprocal-space convergence checks.

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • The initial validation specification is noncoincident point charges in a cubic, triply periodic Coulomb cell with zero net charge and no uniform neutralizing background.
  • Omit only i=j in the same cell (n=0); retain periodic self images and account separately for the self-energy correction.
  • Specify splitting, real cutoff and reciprocal/mesh parameters. The mean-potential gauge and the macroscopic surface convention are different choices.

Specified: Specified here as a pre-implementation comparison convention, not an executed setup.

Boundaries: Triply periodic neutral cell; the initial comparison specifies a conducting (tin-foil) exterior with zero macroscopic surface term and separately fixes the mean-potential gauge. Vacuum-exterior, slab and isolated-boundary results are different problems.

Specified: Specified here as a pre-implementation comparison convention, not an executed setup.

Units: Comparison convention: fixed length ℓ₀, charge q₀ and Coulomb prefactor kₑ; energy unit kₑq₀²/ℓ₀ and force unit kₑq₀²/ℓ₀². The splitting coefficient has inverse-length units. Do not mix reduced kₑ=1 and SI conventions or reuse gravitational-PM signs.
