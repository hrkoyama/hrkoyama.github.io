# Weighted pseudo-arclength continuation / 重み付き擬似弧長継続

Status: educational-example

Follow the lower and upper branches of the same one-dimensional Bratu boundary-value problem; check tangents, the fold and spatial error against an independent analytic family.

同じ1次元Bratu境界値問題の下枝から上枝までを追跡し、接線・fold・独立解析族との空間誤差を調べます。

A small one-dimensional simple fold only. The whole-curve family comparison uses the computed midpoint as its family coordinate and is distinct from the independent fixed-lambda profile checks. The continuum fold is not an exact discrete solution. No general bifurcation-tracking or large-scale performance claim.

Other names: PALC, 擬似弧長法

- Reading (ja): https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/ja/read/validation-bratu-continuation-jfnk/#bratu-fold-continuation
- Reading (en): https://hrkoyama.github.io/numerical-methods-atlas/en/read/validation-bratu-continuation-jfnk/#bratu-fold-continuation



- Reference: https://ipsen.math.ncsu.edu/ps/sinum65438.pdf — Section2.2, printedpp265–266, Eqs2.2–2.5; tangent null equation, unit Euclidean tangent, augmented system and tangent hyperplane. Weighted h-sum metric is the implementation’s stated discrete variation. No full condition-number proof or original paper numerical benchmark is borrowed.
- Reference: https://hplgit.github.io/INF5620/doc/pub/._main_nonlin005.html — Problem8/Eq52: boundary-value equation, analytic cosh family, branch count and printed rounded critical value. The author’s parameter equals2 times our theta. Independent Decimal80 exact-family qualification remains the numerical reference; the printed rounded anchor is a separate coarse check.
