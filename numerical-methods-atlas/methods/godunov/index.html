解く問題と採用形

解く問題
圧縮性流体など、衝撃波を含む1次元保存則の時間発展。
採用した変種・範囲
solvers.solveのmethod='godunov': 区分一定状態＋選択した近似Riemann流束＋SSPRK(3,3)。既定Euler流束はHLLC。

使える条件

  • 高次再構成の効果を測る、保守的な空間1次の比較基準が欲しい。
  • 実装範囲内の1次元モデルで、波速に応じた時間刻みを選べる。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 接触面や滑らかな波を少ないセルで高精度に保ちたい。
  • 真空・乾燥・複雑な境界を、この基準solverだけで保証付きに扱いたい。

解説と位置付け

解説章を読む

前提・比較仕様

  • 対象は1次元・一様Cartesian格子上の双曲型保存則で、各方程式に整合する流束を選ぶ。
  • Eulerでは理想気体、正の密度・圧力を前提とし、HLLC等を選べる。浅水・等温モデルにはHLLまたはRusanovを選ぶ。
  • 公開solve(...,method='godunov')は傾きを0にし、SSPRK(3,3)で時間積分する。初期値は格子点でのサンプルをセル値として与える。
  • 公開solverが受け付けるCFLは0<CFL≤0.5。この上限だけで任意の入力の正値性を保証するものではない。

条件を記載: solveの再構成分岐、対応モデル、CFL検査を確認。

境界条件: 公開_padは周期、端点初期値固定、または端値の外挿でゴースト値を作る。外挿は厳密な無反射条件ではなく、反射壁や一般の流入条件は実装していない。非周期領域の総量変化には境界流束が入る。

条件を記載: _padの3つの分岐を確認。

単位系: 方程式ごとの保存変数と流束に一貫した単位を使う。Eulerでは密度ρ、運動量密度ρu、全エネルギー密度Eで、流束の単位は各保存量×長さ/時間。公開衝撃波管は無次元。

条件を記載: equations.pyの保存変数・物理流束で定義。

適用範囲と残る作業

空間1次なので衝撃波や接触面が広がる。原報の厳密Riemann解を用いる1段更新そのものではなく、時間3次でも全体の空間精度は1次のまま。近似流束・初期値の点サンプル・境界処理の選択に依存し、真空、乾湿境界、一般EOS、多次元および一般的な正値性・エントロピー保証は対象外。

条件を記載: 空間再構成と時間積分を分け、原法との差を明記。

Method-specific conditions and sources: 2026-11-22

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Time evolution of one-dimensional conservation laws, including shocks in compressible flow.
Variant and scope
method='godunov' in solvers.solve: piecewise-constant states, a selected approximate Riemann flux and SSPRK(3,3). HLLC is the default Euler flux.

Use when

  • A conservative first-order spatial baseline is needed to measure the effect of higher-order reconstruction.
  • A supported one-dimensional model can be stepped according to its wave speeds.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Contacts or smooth waves must remain highly accurate on a coarse mesh.
  • Vacuum, dry states or complex boundaries require guarantees absent from this baseline solver.

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • The scope is one-dimensional hyperbolic conservation laws on a uniform Cartesian grid with a model-compatible numerical flux.
  • Euler assumes a perfect gas and positive density/pressure; HLLC and other Euler fluxes are available. Shallow-water and isothermal models require HLL or Rusanov in this solver.
  • solve(...,method='godunov') sets reconstruction slopes to zero and advances with SSPRK(3,3). Initial data are sampled at grid locations and used as cell values.
  • The released solver requires 0<CFL≤0.5; this bound alone is not a positivity guarantee for arbitrary inputs.

Specified: Inspected reconstruction branches, model restrictions and CFL checks in solve.

Boundaries: The released _pad supplies periodic ghosts, fixed initial endpoint states, or edge extrapolation. Extrapolation is not an exact nonreflecting condition; reflecting walls and general inflow conditions are not implemented. Boundary fluxes contribute to global balances on nonperiodic domains.

Specified: Inspected the three _pad branches.

Units: Use units consistent with each model's conserved variables and fluxes. Euler uses density ρ, momentum density ρu and total energy density E; each flux has its conserved-variable unit times length/time. Released shock tubes are nondimensional.

Specified: Specified from conserved variables and physical fluxes in equations.py.

Shared input, execution, validation and citation steps

Scope and remaining work

First-order spatial reconstruction smears shocks and contacts. This is not the original one-step exact-Riemann algorithm, and third-order time stepping does not raise its spatial order. Results depend on the approximate flux, sampled initialization and boundary treatment. Vacuum, wet/dry fronts, general equations of state, multiple dimensions, and general positivity/entropy guarantees are outside scope.

Specified: Distinguishes spatial reconstruction, time integration and the historical algorithm.

Scope of this review

Read solve, rk3 and _pad in solvers.py, Euler/Barotropic in equations.py, and the approximate-flux and exact_euler functions in riemann.py. Inspected the Godunov branch of test_sod_accuracy_and_refinement and reference-Riemann tests. No tests were rerun and no paper tables reproduced.
