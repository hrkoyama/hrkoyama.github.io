比較するときの注意

金属の占有とsmearing幅の依存、有限温度の自由エネルギーと零温度エネルギーを区別します。Γ点だけで十分かは系とセルに依存します。

この例の前提

  • 固定した中性単純立方Heセル・価電子2・スピン非偏極LDA_X＋LDA_C_VWN（VWN5）・選択した局所GTH。
  • Γを含む3次元一様kメッシュの分率座標と重みを入力に明示。
  • 独自のPySCF実行・検証ワークフローであり、自作DFTエンジンではない。独立参照は別実行のDFTK。

境界条件: 3次元Bloch周期境界条件。明示した中性1原子セル・価電子2。

単位系: 原子単位系：bohr、逆bohr、中性基本セル当たりhartree。k点の分率値は逆格子基底での座標。

周期HeセルのDFT：PySCFの実行・収束検査

固定した1原子単純立方Heセルの周期KS-DFT。PySCFを使う教育用ワークフローで、Γ点／k点、Gaussian軌道基底、FFT補助格子、SCF公差を別々に検査し、同じGTHパラメータ・LDA交換＋VWN5相関のDFTK平面波計算と比較。平衡He結晶や一般物質・金属の検証ではありません。

Comparison notes

Distinguish metallic occupation/smearing dependence, finite-temperature free energy and zero-temperature energy. Whether Γ-only sampling is adequate depends on the system and cell.

Assumptions for this example

  • Fixed neutral simple-cubic He cell, two valence electrons, unpolarized LDA_X + LDA_C_VWN (VWN5), selected local GTH potential.
  • Uniform Gamma-centered 3D fractional k meshes and weights are explicit in input.
  • This is an original PySCF driver and validation workflow, not an independently authored DFT engine. Independent reference is separately executed DFTK.

Boundaries: Three-dimensional Bloch periodic boundary conditions; explicit neutral one-atom cell, two electrons.

Units: Atomic units: bohr, inverse bohr, hartree per neutral primitive cell; k fractions use reciprocal-lattice coordinates.

Periodic-He DFT: PySCF execution and convergence checks

Periodic KS-DFT for one fixed simple-cubic He cell. An educational PySCF workflow separately checks Gamma/k sampling, Gaussian orbital basis, auxiliary FFT grid and SCF tolerances against independent DFTK plane waves with identical GTH parameters and LDA exchange plus VWN5 correlation. Does not validate equilibrium helium solids, general materials or metals.

Gamma, k mesh, basis, SCF and independent reference · Python driver · Tests · ZIP

Periodic He₂ forces and the BOMD interface

Asymmetric two-He periodic cell with local GTH and LDA exchange plus VWN5. Compare PySCF analytic forces, including Gaussian-basis derivatives, with energy finite differences for all six coordinates and independent DFTK plane waves. Separate force basis, k-point, SCF and FFT refinement. BOMD coverage is a force interface and short step comparison; long NVE, CPMD and general materials remain unvalidated.

Scope and remaining work

Limited to a selected local GTH and fixed He cell. Nonlocal/PAW/general-element transferability, metals/smearing, a complete-basis limit and agreement with experiment are unvalidated. BZ quadrature meshes are distinct from band paths.
