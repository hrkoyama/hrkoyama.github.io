Dedicated Bratu derivative-comparison supplement

Compare analytic, finite-difference and forward-mode AD derivatives of one discrete residual, separating derivative errors, Newton convergence and spatial refinement. Reverse mode, implicit sensitivities and general speed rankings are outside the implemented scope. This supplement preserves the existing code edition and shared execution registry.

Scope and remaining work

Limited to educational real forward mode and small dense-Newton Bratu comparisons. Reverse mode, HVPs and matrix-free Newton–Krylov remain explanatory only. A separate dedicated supplement verifies implicit sensitivities and discrete adjoints.
