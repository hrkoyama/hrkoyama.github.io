この例の前提

  • 第6版は線形・等方・受動・非磁性板、真空中の一次元垂直入射、単一Lorentz共鳴または導電損失。旧TMz/TEz例も保持。

境界条件: 第6版は遠方PEC外端、PMLなし、半体積平均の整合界面と真空モニター。旧周期/PEC・PML例を保持。

単位系: 無次元。長さ・時間・場を規格化。

Yee FDTDの専用配布版と固定入力

教育用Yee FDTD。真空・Fresnel界面・s/p偏光・PMLの既存例を保持し、第6版で一次元垂直入射のOhmic・受動Lorentz板を追加。帯域解析解、反射・透過・直接散逸・物質蓄積、修正離散収支・物理流束と格子・時間収束を分けて検査。一般分散、多共鳴、斜め分散入射、分散PML、一般2/3次元の包括的検証ではない。

Assumptions for this example

  • New r6: linear isotropic passive nonmagnetic finite slabs in vacuum, 1D normal incidence, one Lorentz resonance or Ohmic conduction. Earlier TMz/TEz cases retained.

Boundaries: New r6: distant PEC caps, no PML, aligned half-volume slab interfaces and vacuum monitors. Earlier periodic/PEC and PML examples retained.

Units: Dimensionless normalized length, time and fields.

Dedicated Yee FDTD edition and fixed inputs

Educational Yee FDTD: retained vacuum/Fresnel/s-p/PML examples plus r6 1D normal-incidence Ohmic and passive Lorentz slabs. Spectral references, R/T, direct dissipation, material storage, modified/physical budgets and space/time refinement are separate checks. Not blanket validation of general dispersion, multiple resonances, oblique dispersive incidence, dispersive PML or general 2D/3D.
