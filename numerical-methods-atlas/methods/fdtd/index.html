この例の前提

  • 指定した無損失・非磁性平面界面の単一ky TEz p偏光波束。既存TMzの真空・s偏光・PML例も保持。

境界条件: 新TEz例：y周期、遠方PEC x外端、PMLなし。単一モードへ厳密縮約し全2次元更新との同値性を検査。既存TMz周期/PEC・左右x/四辺/角PMLも保持。

単位系: 無次元。長さ・時間・場を規格化。

Yee FDTDの専用配布版と固定入力

教育用Yee FDTD。既存TMzの真空・界面・PMLに加え、第5版でTEzのp偏光・Brewster条件・全反射を、指定した単一横モードの波束で検証。一般2次元更新との同値性、連続Fresnel参照、有限窓の反射透過流束・複素振幅・エバネッセント減衰と蓄積、離散保存量・物理収支を区別。一般媒質・多モード・損失分散・3次元の包括的な実証ではない。

Assumptions for this example

  • Selected lossless nonmagnetic planar-interface single-ky TEz p packets; retained TMz vacuum, s-polarized and PML cases.

Boundaries: New TEz cases: periodic y, distant PEC x caps, no PML; exact single-mode Yee reduction with full-2D equivalence tests. Retained TMz periodic/PEC and x/four-side/corner PML cases.

Units: Dimensionless normalized length, time and fields.

Dedicated Yee FDTD edition and fixed inputs

Educational Yee FDTD: retained TMz vacuum/interface/PML cases plus r5 TEz p polarization, Brewster condition and TIR for specified single-transverse-mode packets. Full-2D equivalence, continuum Fresnel data, finite-window flux/amplitudes, evanescent decay/storage, discrete invariants and physical budgets have distinct roles. Not blanket validation of general media, multiple modes, loss/dispersion or 3D.
