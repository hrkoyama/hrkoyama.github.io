この例の前提

  • 選定した真空2次元TMz波束の四辺・角PML。従来の真空モードと垂直入射の無損失誘電体界面も保持。

境界条件: 従来の周期/PEC・左右x方向PMLの例を保持。四辺・角PMLの矩形はすべての外端がPEC。

単位系: 無次元。長さ・時間・場を規格化。

Assumptions for this example

  • Selected vacuum 2D TMz packets with four-side/corner PML, retained vacuum modes and normal-incidence lossless dielectric interface.

Boundaries: Retained periodic/PEC and x-PML cases; four-side/corner PML rectangle has outer PEC caps on every side.

Units: Dimensionless normalized length, time and fields.

Dedicated Yee FDTD edition and fixed inputs

Educational 2D TMz: original vacuum modes, r2 normal dielectric interface/x-PML, and r3 four-side/four-corner PML for specified packets, grids and observation windows. Continuum references, discrete Fourier references, self-convergence, independently tallied work and physical flux have distinct roles. Not blanket validation of general matter, arbitrary incidence or 3D.
