Hartree–Fock法

教育用例・関連コードあり

単一Slater行列式により交換を含む平均場を求める。公開実装は球対称He 1s²の閉殻解に限り、1個の空間軌道で成立する局所交換への簡約を用いる。

解く問題と採用形

解く問題
交換を含む単一行列式近似のHe閉殻基底状態を求める。 別実装のH₂分子RHFも学ぶ。
採用した変種・範囲
同一1s空間軌道を2電子が占有する動径HF。占有軌道上での交換簡約によりv_eff=−2/r+v_H/2。 H₂側の分子積分・行列SCFは別配布版。

使える条件

  • He 1s²で直接項・交換項・二重計数補正の関係を学ぶ。
  • 同じ非相対論RHFモデルの格子解と独立Gaussian基底参照を比較する。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 多軌道、開殻、分子、周期系へ局所交換−v_H/2を流用する。
  • 相関エネルギーや多参照性をこのHF値で説明する。

比較するときの注意

HFの解は一般に一意とは限りません。SCFが停止しても基底収束・軌道安定性・正しい電子状態の確認が必要です。分子解離では単一参照の限界が現れます。

前提・比較仕様

  • 従来の原子例：Z=2の孤立He、無限質量の点核、非相対論。逆スピンの2電子が同じ球対称1s空間軌道を占有する。
  • 従来の原子例：∫|u|²dr=1とし、p=2|u|²=4πr²n、∫p dr=2と定義する。
  • 従来の原子例：この占有軌道に限り有効場は−2/r+v_H/2、交換エネルギーはE_x=−E_H/2に簡約される。一般の非局所Fock演算子は実装していない。
  • 従来の原子例：単一行列式の交換を含み、電子相関を含まない。
  • 別の分子例：H₂の有限s型Gaussian基底RHF。自作の1・2電子積分と行列SCFを用い、公差・収束はH₂入力と検証結果で確認する。

条件を記載: helium('hf')の占有・p=2u²・交換簡約を確認。

境界条件: He縮約軌道にu(0)=u(r_max)=0（基本r_max=16 bohr、別途4/8/12/16 bohrの領域検査）。Hartree場は原点で有限、密度の外ではN/rへ接続し、r_maxで0には固定しません。 別のH₂側は孤立分子の全空間Gaussian解析積分で、有限の動径箱は設けない。

条件を記載: 軌道Dirichlet条件と孤立系Hartree境界を区別。

単位系: 原子単位系（ℏ=mₑ=e=4πε₀=1）。rはbohr、エネルギー・有効ポテンシャルはhartree、規格化した縮約軌道uはbohr⁻¹ᐟ²。正の電子数密度n(r)はbohr⁻³、動径電子数密度p(r)=4πr²n(r)はbohr⁻¹、∫p dr=N。pは符号付き電荷密度ではありません。 分子側：3次元AOはbohr^(−3/2)で規格化。重なり行列・AO密度行列・電子数は無次元、直交化したFock交換子はhartree。分子側の公差は別に記載。

条件を記載: u、p、nとエネルギー成分の規約を確認。

適用範囲と残る作業

Hartree–Fock

Educational example / related code

Find an exchange-containing mean field in a single Slater determinant. The released implementation is restricted to spherical closed-shell helium 1s² and uses the local reduction valid for one occupied spatial orbital.

Method mapCode, inputs and checksReading chapters

Method-specific conditions and sources: 2026-11-22

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Find the closed-shell helium ground state in a single-determinant approximation with exchange. A separate H₂ molecular RHF example extends the scope.
Variant and scope
Radial HF for two electrons occupying one common 1s spatial orbital; exchange acting on that orbital reduces v_eff to -2/r+v_H/2. Molecular integrals/matrix SCF for H₂ are in a separate edition.

Use when

  • Study Coulomb, exchange and double-counting terms in helium 1s².
  • Compare the grid solution with an independent Gaussian-basis reference for the same nonrelativistic RHF model.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Reusing local exchange -v_H/2 for multi-orbital, open-shell, molecular or periodic systems.
  • Using this HF value to describe correlation energy or multireference physics.

Comparison notes

HF solutions need not be unique. SCF termination does not prove basis convergence, orbital stability or the desired electronic state. Bond dissociation can expose single-reference limitations.

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • Atomic branch: Nonrelativistic isolated He with Z=2, an infinite-mass point nucleus and two opposite-spin electrons occupying the same spherical 1s spatial orbital.
  • Atomic branch: Normalize integral |u|^2 dr=1 and define p=2|u|^2=4 pi r^2 n, so integral p dr=2.
  • Atomic branch: For this occupied orbital only, the Fock equation reduces to the effective field -2/r+v_H/2 and E_x=-E_H/2; no general nonlocal Fock operator is implemented.
  • Atomic branch: Single-determinant exchange is included; electron correlation is omitted.
  • Separate molecular branch: H2 RHF in finite s-Gaussian spaces, analytic one-/two-electron integrals and matrix SCF; see the H2 input and measured checks for its own tolerances.

Specified: Checked occupations, p=2u^2 and the exchange reduction in helium('hf').

Boundaries: Helium reduced orbital: u(0)=u(r_max)=0, baseline r_max=16 bohr with a separate 4/8/12/16-bohr domain study. The Hartree field is finite at the origin and joins N/r outside the density; it is not fixed to zero at r_max. Separate H2 branch: isolated molecule, analytic whole-space Gaussian integrals; no finite radial box.

Specified: Distinguished orbital Dirichlet conditions from isolated Hartree boundaries.

Units: Atomic units (hbar=m_e=e=4 pi epsilon_0=1): r in bohr, energies/effective potentials in hartree, and normalized reduced orbitals u in bohr^(-1/2). Positive electron-number density n(r) is in bohr^(-3); radial number density p(r)=4 pi r^2 n(r) is in bohr^(-1), with integral p dr=N. p is not signed charge density. Molecular branch: normalized 3D AO functions in bohr^(-3/2); overlap, AO density matrix and electron count dimensionless; transformed Fock commutator in hartree. Molecular tolerances are stated separately.

Specified: Checked conventions for u, p, n and energy components.

Scope and remaining work

The local exchange reduction cannot be transferred unchanged to multi-orbital, open-shell, molecular or periodic systems. The atomic edition does not implement UHF/ROHF, molecular integrals or orbital-stability analysis. Saved PySCF RHF references are independent finite-Gaussian-basis calculations, not exact correlated helium energies. Separate grid, domain and SCF convergence from physical model error. H₂ uses a separate molecular-integral implementation. Molecular DFT, general multicenter molecules, open shells and orbital-stability analysis remain unimplemented.

Specified: Explicitly excludes claims of a general Fock solver, correlation or stability analysis.
