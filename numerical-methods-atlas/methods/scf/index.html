解く問題と採用形

解く問題
指定した電子密度依存ハミルトニアンと密度の自己整合固定点を求め、未収束を識別する。 別実装のH₂分子RHFも学ぶ。
採用した変種・範囲
単純線形密度混合。密度L1差・エネルギー変化・出力密度で再構成した軌道残差のAND条件、履歴保存、反復上限による明示的未収束。 H₂側の分子積分・行列SCFは別配布版。

使える条件

  • 小さなHeモデルで混合率・初期値・停止基準を比較する。
  • 正常終了と数値収束を区別し、反復上限1回の意図的な未収束を扱う。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 金属・難収束系や汎用分子SCFで、この混合率と公差が常に適切とみなす。
  • 反復が停止した事実だけで安定性・最低エネルギー・物理精度を保証する。

境界条件: 原子・分子の実行例：SCF混合そのものに空間境界条件はない。与えたHe固定点写像が縮約軌道u(0)=u(r_max)=0と、原点で有限・遠方N/rの孤立Hartree場を規定する。HF・KS・Hartree項目を参照。 別のH₂側は孤立分子の全空間Gaussian解析積分で、有限の動径箱は設けない。 周期He：固定1原子単純立方セルの3次元Bloch境界。

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Find a self-consistent fixed point of a specified density-dependent Hamiltonian and identify failure. A separate H₂ molecular RHF example extends the scope.
Variant and scope
Simple linear density mixing; joint density-L1, energy-change and output-density-rebuilt orbital-residual criteria, saved histories and explicit iteration-limit failure. Molecular integrals/matrix SCF for H₂ are in a separate edition.

Use when

  • Compare mixing rates, starts and stopping criteria in a small helium model.
  • Distinguish process completion from convergence, including an intentional one-iteration failure.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Assuming the same mixing and tolerances are generally suitable for metals, difficult systems or molecular SCF.
  • Using iteration termination alone to establish stability, minimum energy or physical accuracy.

Comparison notes

A small energy change alone is insufficient. Convergence also does not establish the lowest-energy stable electronic solution.

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • Atomic/molecular examples: Earlier helium branch: The fixed-point map is the released spherical He 1s² HF or exchange-only LDA radial model, not a general molecular or periodic SCF implementation.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: Earlier helium branch: Mix input and output radial number densities: p_next=(1-alpha)p_in+alpha p_out, 0<alpha<=1; released baseline alpha=0.35 and an alternative start uses 0.7.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: Earlier helium branch: Each eigenproblem normalizes u and sets p_out=2|u|^2, so the electron-number normalization is imposed within the iteration.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: Earlier helium branch: Convergence requires input/output density L1<1e-9 electrons, |energy change|<1e-10 hartree, and the output-density-rebuilt orbital residual<1e-7 hartree; maximum 300 iterations.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: Earlier helium branch: The atlas iteration implements no DIIS, Anderson or Newton acceleration. An independent PySCF reference may use its own solver algorithms.
  • Atomic/molecular examples: Separate molecular branch: H2 RHF in finite s-Gaussian spaces, analytic one-/two-electron integrals and matrix SCF; see the H2 input and measured checks for its own tolerances.
  • Periodic KS-DFT for one fixed simple-cubic He cell. An educational PySCF workflow separately checks Gamma/k sampling, Gaussian orbital basis, auxiliary FFT grid and SCF tolerances against independent DFTK plane waves with identical GTH parameters and LDA exchange plus VWN5 correlation. Specified: Inspected helium's density update, three stopping criteria and iteration cap.

Boundaries: Atomic/molecular examples: SCF mixing itself imposes no spatial boundary condition. The supplied helium fixed-point map uses reduced-orbital u(0)=u(r_max)=0 and an isolated finite-origin, N/r Hartree field; see the HF/KS/Hartree records. Not applicable to this component: Mixing has no intrinsic spatial boundary; the helium fixed-point map supplies it.

Units: Atomic/molecular examples: Mixing alpha and iteration number are dimensionless. Radial p is in bohr^(-1), integral |p_out-p_in| dr is electron number, and energy changes/normalized orbital residuals are in hartree. Published tolerances belong to this normalization and model. Molecular branch: normalized 3D AO functions in bohr^(-3/2); overlap, AO density matrix and electron count dimensionless; transformed Fock commutator in hartree. Molecular tolerances are stated separately. Periodic He: bohr, inverse bohr and hartree per neutral primitive cell; reciprocal-lattice fractional k coordinates.
