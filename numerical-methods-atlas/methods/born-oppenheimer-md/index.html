この例の前提

  • 核は古典力学、電子は選択した断熱的基底状態。固定He₂の力供給と局所Verlet確認があるが、軌道全体は未検証。

Comparison notes

Overall BOMD remains reading only. The separate force release’s short Verlet interface check does not validate long trajectories or thermostats.

Assumptions for this example

  • Classical nuclei and a selected adiabatic electronic ground state. A fixed-He₂ force-provider/local-Verlet check exists, while full trajectories remain unvalidated.
  • The published interface fixes the periodic cell, occupations and electronic model, and reconverges SCF at every visited geometry.

Boundaries: Choose isolated or periodic boundaries for the model; the published local interface uses the fixed periodic He₂ cell.

Units: Atomic units: energies hartree, forces hartree/bohr, positions bohr, time atomic units and explicit nuclear masses in electron masses.

Scope and remaining work

Overall BOMD remains reading only. The separate force release’s short Verlet interface check does not validate long trajectories or thermostats.
