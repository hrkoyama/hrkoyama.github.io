解く問題と採用形

解く問題
セル界面の左右Euler状態から、接触波を含む近似保存流束を求める。
採用した変種・範囲
riemann.numerical_flux(...,solver='hllc')。Davis型外側波速、左右star状態、接触波を使う理想気体Euler版。

使える条件

  • 正の理想気体状態から、HLLより鮮明な密度接触面を求めたい。
  • 再構成・時間積分・境界条件を別に選ぶGodunov型solverの流束部品が必要。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 真空や極端な希薄波に対して正値性の保証が必須。
  • 非理想気体、浅水、MHD等のための別のHLLC定式化が必要。

解説と位置付け

解説章を読む

前提・比較仕様

  • 左右状態は理想気体Eulerの(ρ,ρu,E)、有限かつρ>0、p>0で、物理的な比熱比γ>1を使う。
  • 外側波速はS_L=min(u_L−c_L,u_R−c_R)、S_R=max(u_L+c_L,u_R+c_R)。接触速度と左右star状態を代数的に計算する。
  • HLLCは界面流束であり、再構成、時間積分、CFLと境界状態は呼び出し側が与える。
  • 本実装のHLLC分岐はEuler専用であり、HLLを使える他のモデルにも自動適用できるわけではない。

条件を記載: numerical_fluxのEuler型検査と波速・star状態の式を確認。

境界条件: 界面流束そのものには物理境界条件を指定しない。呼び出し元が左右状態を作る。既存1次元solverは周期・端点初期値固定・端値外挿を用いる。

この構成要素では対象外: 流束関数は左右状態を受け取り、境界を設定しない。

単位系: 左右で同じ単位系を使う。ρは密度、uとcと各波速は長さ/時間、pとEはエネルギー/体積。数値流束は保存量×長さ/時間の単位を持つ。γは無次元で、公開例も無次元。

条件を記載: Eulerの保存変数と波速の次元から指定。

Method-specific conditions and sources: 2026-11-22

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Compute a contact-resolving approximate conservative flux from left/right Euler states.
Variant and scope
riemann.numerical_flux(...,solver='hllc'): perfect-gas Euler HLLC with Davis-type outer speeds, two star states and a contact wave.

Use when

  • Density contacts should be sharper than with HLL for positive perfect-gas states.
  • A flux component is needed in a Godunov-type solver that separately selects reconstruction, time integration and boundaries.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Guaranteed positivity is required near vacuum or extreme rarefactions.
  • A different HLLC formulation is needed for nonideal gases, shallow water or MHD.

Read the formulation

Read the chapter

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • Left/right states are perfect-gas Euler variables (ρ,ρu,E), finite with ρ>0 and p>0, using a physical γ>1.
  • Outer speeds are S_L=min(u_L−c_L,u_R−c_R) and S_R=max(u_L+c_L,u_R+c_R); the contact speed and two star states are computed algebraically.
  • HLLC supplies an interface flux. Reconstruction, time integration, CFL control and boundary states are provided by the caller.
  • The local HLLC branch accepts Euler only; availability of HLL for another model does not make this HLLC implementation applicable to it.

Specified: Inspected the Euler type restriction and wave-speed/star-state formulae.

Boundaries: Physical boundary conditions do not belong to the interface-flux function. The caller constructs left/right states; the existing one-dimensional solver uses periodic, fixed-initial-endpoint or extrapolated-edge states.

Not applicable to this component: The flux function accepts two states and does not impose boundaries.

Units: Use one consistent unit system on both sides. ρ is density, u, c and wave speeds are length/time, and p and E are energy/volume. Numerical fluxes have conserved-variable units times length/time. γ is dimensionless; released examples are nondimensional.

Specified: Specified from Euler conserved variables and wave-speed dimensions.

Shared input, execution, validation and citation steps

Scope and remaining work

Contact restoration is not a general positivity or entropy-stability guarantee. This Davis-speed version should not be extended without additional treatment to strong rarefactions, vacuum, general equations of state, MHD or relativistic flow. Exact agreement for a stationary contact does not imply an exact solution for moving shocks or arbitrary problems.

Specified: Separates a local stationary-contact test from general guarantees.

Read the HLL/HLLC branches of numerical_flux, the non-vacuum check and bracketed pressure bisection in exact_euler. Inspected flux consistency, stationary-contact and Sod reference/accuracy tests. No rerun was performed.
