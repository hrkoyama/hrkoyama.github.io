低Mach数のPDE前処理

教育用例・関連コードあり

低Mach数での音響・対流尺度と、擬似時間の質量行列または数値散逸の前処理を区別します。専用の軽量例は、物理時間を保つMiczek系Roe散逸行列の実装と通常Roeを比較します。

解説と位置付け

解説章を読む

この例の前提

  • Smooth fixed isentropic-vortex data for the unforced 2D ideal-gas Euler equations; gamma=1.4.
  • Matrix preconditioning is applied to numerical dissipation, retaining the physical-time mass matrix.
  • Only the stated fixed Mach numbers, grids, acoustic CFL and short time interval are validated.

境界条件: Periodic finite box. The whole-plane Gaussian analytic vortex has tiny nonzero minimum-image seam tails, which are explicitly qualified.

単位系: Nondimensional: far-field density1, background velocity(1,.5), beta3, box[-10,10]^2; pressure scales consistently with the chosen background Mach number.
