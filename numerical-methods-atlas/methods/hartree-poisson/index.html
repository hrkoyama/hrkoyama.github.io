Coulomb・Hartreeポテンシャル

教育用例・関連コードあり

正の電子数密度から電子間反発のHartree場とエネルギーを求める。公開実装は球対称密度のCoulomb積分を内側・外側の累積和で評価し、汎用の3次元Poissonソルバーは用いない。

手法・コードの対応表コード・入力・検証分野別に解説を読む

別名・表記：Hartree potential / 電子密度 / Coulomb

条件・文献の個別整理：2026-11-22

解く問題と採用形

解く問題
与えた球対称電子数密度が作る孤立系Coulomb反発場とHartreeエネルギーを求める。
採用した変種・範囲
動径密度pに対する内側・外側積分の累積求積。Poisson式は符号と境界の等価な記述で、専用PDE反復解法ではない。

使える条件

  • 球対称の滑らかな電子密度でv_HとE_Hを評価する。
  • 解析的指数密度と遠方の電子数/rでCoulomb場の符号・境界・会計を確認する。

避ける条件・追加処理が必要な場合

  • 非球対称・周期系にこの1次元Coulomb積分をそのまま適用する。
  • 同じ求積の標本を1回にする処理を、点電荷自己エネルギー除去や交換と混同する。

解説と位置付け

解説章を読む

比較するときの注意

Poisson解の残差、積分した電子数、遠方の挙動、二重計数の1/2を別々に確かめます。周期セルの帯電系を孤立系のエネルギーとして解釈しません。

前提・比較仕様

  • 非負・球対称の電子数密度n(r)を仮定。p(r)=4πr²n(r)、∫p dr=Nと定義し、公開He例はN=2。
  • 孤立系のv_H(r)=∫₀ᴿp(s)/max(r,s)dsとE_H=(1/2)∫p(r)v_H(r)drを評価する。
  • 一様格子の累積求積を用い、両端p=0、有限箱外の密度は0とする。同じ半径の求積標本を1回だけ数える。
  • v_Hは正の電子間反発で、原子単位系で∇²v_H=−4πn。符号付き電子電荷密度は−nであり、v_Hは負電荷が作る静電ポテンシャルそのものではなく電子のポテンシャルエネルギー。

条件を記載: hartree_potentialの入力は符号付き電荷ではなくp=4πr²n。

境界条件: 孤立・球対称Coulomb境界。v_Hは原点で有限、切断した密度の外側ではN/r。r_maxで0とするDirichlet条件ではない。端点p=0は縮約軌道から与え、周期系の中性化・背景電荷の規約は実装していない。

条件を記載: 原点有限・密度外N/rを確認。軌道のu=0境界とは異なる。

単位系: 原子単位系。rはbohr、nはbohr⁻³、pはbohr⁻¹、Nは電子数、v_HとE_Hはhartree。正の電子数密度という規約とPoisson式の符号を組にする。

条件を記載: 数密度・動径密度・反発ポテンシャルの単位と符号を指定。

Coulomb and Hartree potentials

Educational example / related code

Compute the repulsive Hartree field and energy from positive electron-number density. The released implementation evaluates spherical Coulomb integrals using inward/outward cumulative sums, not a general three-dimensional Poisson solver.

Methods and code referenceCode, inputs and checksLearn by field

Other names: Hartree potential / 電子密度 / Coulomb

Method-specific conditions and sources: 2026-11-22

Problem and adopted formulation

Problem
Find the isolated repulsive Coulomb field and Hartree energy of a specified spherical electron-number density.
Variant and scope
Cumulative inner/outer radial quadrature in p; Poisson's equation provides the equivalent sign/boundary interpretation, not a separate iterative PDE solver.

Use when

  • Evaluate v_H and E_H for smooth spherical electron densities.
  • Check sign, boundaries and accounting against analytic exponential density and the N/r far field.

Avoid or add further treatment when

  • Applying this one-dimensional Coulomb integral directly to nonspherical or periodic systems.
  • Confusing quadrature sample counting with point-charge self-energy removal or exchange.

Read the formulation

Read the chapter

Comparison notes

Check the Poisson residual, integrated electron number, far-field behavior and the one-half double-counting factor separately. A charged periodic cell does not directly give an isolated-system energy.

Assumptions and comparison specification

  • A nonnegative spherically symmetric electron-number density n(r), with p(r)=4 pi r^2 n(r) and integral p dr=N; released helium uses N=2.
  • For an isolated system, v_H(r)=integral_0^R p(s)/max(r,s) ds and E_H=(1/2) integral p(r)v_H(r) dr.
  • The code uses uniform-grid cumulative quadrature with p=0 at the two endpoints and zero density outside the finite box; the same-radius quadrature sample is counted once.
  • v_H is positive electron repulsion and satisfies Laplacian(v_H)=-4 pi n in atomic units. Signed electronic charge density is -n; v_H is electron potential energy, not the electrostatic potential generated by that negative charge.

Specified: hartree_potential takes p=4 pi r^2 n, not signed charge.

Boundaries: Isolated spherical Coulomb boundary: v_H is finite at the origin and equals N/r outside the truncated density support. It is not a zero-Dirichlet condition at r_max. Endpoint p=0 is inherited from the reduced orbital; no periodic neutrality/background convention is implemented.

Specified: Checked finite-origin/N/r behavior, distinct from the orbital's u=0 boundaries.

Units: Atomic units: r in bohr, n in bohr^(-3), p in bohr^(-1), N an electron number, v_H and E_H in hartree. The positive-number-density convention fixes the Poisson sign.

Specified: Specified number/radial density and repulsive-potential units/sign.

Runnable atomic examples

Cumulative Coulomb quadrature for isolated spherical densities, checked against analytic exponential densities; not a general 3D or periodic Poisson implementation.

  • Helium ground state: closed-shell HF and exchange-only LDA

Shared input, execution, validation and citation steps

Scope and remaining work

An integral evaluator for spherical isolated densities truncated in a finite box. It does not treat nonspherical/general boundaries, periodicity, Ewald/background charges or singular point-charge self-energies. Avoiding a double-counted same-radius sample is quadrature bookkeeping, not removal of DFT self-interaction; the helium-HF cancellation comes from the separate exchange reduction.

Specified: Distinguished spherical quadrature, diagonal samples and physical self-interaction.

Scope of this review

This inspection reads the released implementation, tests and saved results; it does not rerun the calculation. Inspected hartree_potential and analytic exponential-density, Hartree-energy and far-field-charge tests, plus saved helium energy accounting.

Not checked or not included
