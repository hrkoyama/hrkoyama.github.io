分野ごとの小規模実装・検証をたどる

共通22実行例の台帳とは別に管理する追加検証集です。配布ZIPを独立展開し、各グループの全固定入力と検査を実行した記録を示します。設定・手法・刻み幅の違いを独立した物理問題の数には加算しません。

各ケースの条件・採用変種・独立参照または解析解・残差・収束・失敗の扱いを確認します。共通22実行例とは別の補助配布版です。量子DFTの全走査は通常の軽量確認へ含めません。

通常の補助例CIはNumPy・SciPy・pytestで実行する9グループです。独立ライブラリ等の保存参照は版と入力を記録した保存値に照合します。量子収束を含む従来の共通22例は必要時の手動実行とし、既存の配布物・公差・履歴を保持します。

33の固定検証例を、9つの配布版から実行できます。

AMG・行列分解・固有値・求積

29検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • 行列から粗視化するAMG：残差と解誤差を分ける

    正方形Poisson問題から得たSPD行列だけを階層構築へ渡し、平滑化集約AMGのV-cycleを実行します。7²・15²・31²未知数で独立疎行列直接解、解析的な離散正弦モード、連続解への2次収束を分けて検査します。

  • Householder QRと片側Jacobi SVD：直交性・階数・最小二乗

    実の縦長行列に対するHouseholder QRと片側Jacobi SVDを自作し、通常行列・列スケール差10⁶の行列・階数落ち行列を検査します。独立LAPACKの特異値と最小ノルム最小二乗解を照合します。

  • Lanczos・Arnoldi：Ritz値と固有対残差の収束

    32・48次の対称／非対称行列でLanczosとArnoldiを自作し、Krylov次元4・8・16による最大実部の固有対の収束を測ります。行列全体を解く独立LAPACK参照と照合します。

  • Gauss–Legendreと適応求積：厳密次数・推定誤差・予算切れ

    Legendreの3項漸化式からGauss節点と重みを構成し、2n−1次までの多項式モーメントと指数関数を検査します。適応Simpsonは指数関数とLorentz型ピークを積分し、解析積分・独立QUADPACKと比較します。

MCMC・温度制御・生物モデル

26検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • Gaussian標的のMCMC：分布・自己相関・ESS

    32本のMetropolis連鎖を既知の標準正規分布へ照合し、平均・分散・CDF、自己相関時間、ESSと標本予算依存を記録します。

  • Langevin温度制御：正準分布・刻み偏り・時間相関

    調和振動子のBAOABを、正準分布と離散Lyapunov定常分布、連続・離散の時間相関へ照合します。

  • 反応拡散：空間2次と時間4次を分けて測る

    線形の拡散・反応演算子を、連続と半離散のFourier厳密解を用いて小規模に検証します。

  • Turing不安定性：線形成長率と小振幅パターン

    Schnakenberg型反応拡散で、一様平衡の安定性と拡散による選択モードの成長を行列指数で検証します。

  • FitzHugh–Nagumo：パルス興奮・回復と独立ODE参照

    2変数の膜興奮縮約ODEを電流パルスで励起し、DOP853との全出力時刻の誤差・時間収束・回復を確認します。

粒子・静電場・運動論・放射輸送

27検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • 中性周期セルのEwald和と独立theta積分

    3次元周期Coulomb相互作用の実空間和・逆空間和・自己項を実装し、分割パラメータと打切りを変えて検証します。

  • Bスプラインsmooth PMEの格子・次数収束

    電荷をBスプラインで格子へ割り当て、FFTでEwald逆空間項を評価するsmooth PME（SPME）の小規模例です。

  • 2次元対数核のM2M・M2L・L2Lを持つFMM

    自由空間の対数ポテンシャルを一様四分木の多重極・局所展開で評価し、直接和と比較します。

  • CIC静電PICの冷たいプラズマ振動

    1次元周期セルの電子をCICで格子と結び、Poisson方程式と粒子運動を自洽に進めます。

  • 等質DSMC衝突成分の保存則・Maxwell分布

    等質なMaxwell分子のランダム二体衝突で、保存則と平衡分布への緩和を検証します。

  • 純吸収平板の離散座標放射輸送

    純吸収の定常放射輸送を角度と空間で離散化し、Beer–Lambert解と積分された出射フラックスで検証します。

流体・MHDの独立照合とCT／GLM

11検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • Orszag–Tang：CT・GLMと独立Athena++比較

    同じ周期Orszag–Tang初期値で、CTとGLMの密度・磁場、保存量、div Bを測り、別に実行したAthena++と比較します。

  • Brio–Wu：別実装HLLDとの照合

    公開HLLD-MC有限体積コードを、固定コミットから別に構築したAthena++のBrio–Wu参照と比較します。

  • Shu–Osher：独立参照と格子収束

    衝撃波と密度正弦波の相互作用を、同じPython実装の細格子ではなく、別に実行したAthena++の参照と照合します。

  • GLMの磁気単極子モード：解析的減衰

    GLM cleaning単独の縦磁場Fourierモードを、連続線形方程式の行列指数解と比較します。

CSVRとLangevin：分布・揺らぎ・相関

42検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • CSVRの厳密浴：条件付き分布・揺らぎ・運動エネルギー相関

    自由度1・2・6のCSVR浴だけを実装し、厳密な条件付きモーメントと非心χ²分布、正準Gamma分布と運動エネルギー相関を照合します。

  • CSVRとLangevin：調和2自由度の分布・揺らぎ・相関

    異なる周波数を持つ2自由度でglobal CSVRとlocal Langevinを同じOBABO分割で比較。位置・運動量・温度・全エネルギーの分布、揺らぎ、相関を記録します。

  • 非調和CSVRとLangevin：Boltzmann求積との分布比較

    弱い四次ポテンシャルを持つ2自由度で、CSVRとLangevinの分布・温度とエネルギーの揺らぎ・相関を比較。正準分布を独立なBoltzmann求積から得ます。

非線形反応拡散・パターン形成の独立検証

28検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • Schnakenberg有限振幅過渡：非線形性と独立参照

    t=8までの有限振幅反応拡散を、独立なFourier–Galerkin参照・格子と時間刻み収束・新しい高調波で検証します。

  • Schnakenberg飽和パターン：近定常性と格子・時間収束

    t=100の1D選択パターンを独立参照で照合。参照の90→100の変化・全PDE残差と、有限刻みprimaryのFD残差を分けて測ります。

多峰性MCMC：混合不良と収束診断の独立検証

39検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • 多峰性MCMC：峰への閉じ込め・偽の安心・大域提案

    同じ2峰密度を、分散初期値の局所MH・同じ峰からの局所MH・対称reflectionを混ぜるMHで比較。期待した混合不良の検出と、実際の峰間移動を分けて検証します。

  • MCMC診断の独立照合：rank Rhat・bulk/tail ESS・退化条件

    同一鎖の62datasetを別環境ArviZ0.20.0で実計算し、固定recipe・入力・鎖・公式ソースSHAで結びます。通常の有限値照合と、不定値のpolicy差を分けて検査します。

CTとGLM：同条件の独立照合・格子・時間収束

46検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • CT・GLM：移動する磁気静力学平衡の格子・時間収束

    非零の磁気力と圧力勾配が釣り合う構成厳密解を使い、同じ初期セル状態・流束の速度上界・刻み列でCT/GLMを比較します。

  • Orszag–Tang：条件を揃えたCT・GLMと独立参照の収束

    同じOT初期セル平均と同じdt列でCT/GLMを計算し、別に実行したAthena++の格子・時間感度を分離した参照と比較します。

  • CTの初期制約保持とGLMの能動的cleaning：非物理診断対照

    故意にdiv B≠0とした同じcell磁場で、CTが初期違反を除去しないこととGLMのB/ψ応答を確認します。物理問題数へ加算しない診断対照です。

基盤手法：条件数・残差・収束と独立参照

49検査に合格。ZIP独立展開から全入力を再実行。

  • AMGの条件数・異方性・残差と前進誤差

    49・225・961未知数のSPD行列で、条件数と異方性を別々に変えます。解析スペクトル、独立疎行列直接解、DST対角化を比較し、同じ残差でも解誤差が条件数に依存することを確かめます。

  • QR/SVD：既定特異値・摂動増幅・数値階数

    二進で厳密に表現できる16×4行列の条件数を1から2³⁶まで変え、再構成誤差と解誤差を分けます。実際のbinary64入力に対し別環境で実行した80/120桁参照とLAPACKを比較します。

  • Krylov固有値：クラスター部分空間と非正規感度

    48次のクラスター固有値と強い非正規上三角行列で、次元4/8/16/32のLanczos・Arnoldiを測ります。残差・前進誤差・固有値条件数・不変部分空間を分けます。

  • 求積：狭いピーク・相殺・端点・誤差推定の盲点

    Gauss次数と適応公差を走査し、狭いLorentzピーク、平方根端点、巨大な相殺、零積分、見逃すcompact bumpを解析積分とQUADPACKで検証します。

Explore small implemented validations by field

These additional validation supplements are managed separately from the common 22-example registry. Receipts cover independent ZIP extraction, tests and every fixed input within each group. Changes of settings, methods or step sizes do not add independent physical problems.

Check each case’s assumptions, adopted variant, independent/analytic reference, residuals, refinement and failure handling. These supplements are separate from the shared 22 execution registry. Detailed quantum-DFT sweeps are outside routine lightweight checks.

Routine supplement CI runs nine NumPy/SciPy/pytest groups, comparing stored independent-library references with versioned, input-traced stored values. The existing common 22-case suite, including quantum convergence, remains manual on explicit need; released artifacts, criteria and history are preserved.

33 fixed validation cases are runnable through 9 supplements.

AtlasField validation scopeJSONMarkdown

AMG, factorizations, eigenvalues and quadrature

29 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Matrix-based AMG: distinguish residual and solution error

    Build a smoothed-aggregation hierarchy from an SPD matrix alone and run V-cycles. Problems with 7², 15² and 31² unknowns distinguish independent sparse direct solutions, analytic discrete sine modes and second-order convergence to the continuum solution.

  • Householder QR and one-sided Jacobi SVD: orthogonality, rank and least squares

    Original Householder QR and one-sided Jacobi SVD for real tall matrices are checked on ordinary, 10⁶-column-scaled and rank-deficient inputs, against separate LAPACK singular values and minimum-norm least-squares solutions.

  • Lanczos and Arnoldi: convergence of Ritz values and eigenpair residuals

    Original Lanczos and Arnoldi on symmetric/nonsymmetric 32×32 and 48×48 matrices measure convergence of the largest-real-part eigenpair over Krylov dimensions 4, 8 and 16, against independent full-matrix LAPACK references.

  • Gauss–Legendre and adaptive quadrature: exactness, error estimates and budgets

    Construct Gaussian nodes/weights from the Legendre recurrence and test polynomial moments through degree 2n−1 and an exponential. Adaptive Simpson integrates exponential and Lorentzian peaks, against analytic integrals and independent QUADPACK.

MCMC, temperature control and biological models

26 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Gaussian-target MCMC: distribution, autocorrelation and ESS

    Check 32 Metropolis chains against a known standard-normal target; record mean, variance, CDF, autocorrelation time, ESS and sample-budget dependence.

  • Langevin thermostat: canonical distributions, timestep bias and correlation

    Compare harmonic BAOAB with canonical and discrete-Lyapunov stationary distributions, and with continuous/discrete time correlations.

  • Reaction-diffusion: separate second-order space and fourth-order time

    Validate a small linear diffusion-reaction operator against continuous and semidiscrete Fourier solutions.

  • Turing instability: linear growth and small-amplitude patterns

    Use a matrix exponential to validate stable homogeneous kinetics and diffusion-driven growth of a selected Schnakenberg mode.

  • FitzHugh-Nagumo: pulse excitation, recovery and an independent ODE reference

    Excite a two-variable reduced membrane ODE with a current pulse; check all-output-time errors, temporal refinement and recovery against DOP853.

Particles, electrostatics, kinetic and radiative transport

27 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Neutral-cell Ewald sums and an independent theta integral

    Implement real-space, reciprocal-space and self Ewald terms for neutral 3D periodic Coulomb charges; vary splitting and cutoffs.

  • Mesh/order convergence of B-spline smooth PME

    A small smooth-PME example assigns charge with B-splines and evaluates the Ewald reciprocal contribution by FFT.

  • 2D logarithmic FMM with M2M, M2L and L2L

    Evaluate a free-space log potential with uniform-quadtree multipole and local expansions and compare with direct sums.

  • Cold-plasma oscillation with electrostatic CIC PIC

    Couple electrons to a 1D periodic grid by CIC, solving Poisson and particle motion self-consistently.

  • Conservation and Maxwell relaxation in a homogeneous DSMC collision component

    Test conservation and relaxation toward equilibrium through randomized pair collisions of homogeneous Maxwell molecules.

  • Discrete-ordinate radiation transport through an absorbing slab

    Discretize steady pure-absorption transfer in angle and space, checking Beer–Lambert rays and integrated exit flux.

Independent fluid/MHD comparisons and CT/GLM

11 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Orszag–Tang: CT, GLM and independent Athena++

    Compare CT and GLM density, magnetic field, conservation and div B for the same periodic Orszag–Tang data, against separately executed Athena++.

  • Brio–Wu against an independent HLLD implementation

    Compare the published HLLD-MC finite-volume code with Brio–Wu reference data independently produced by Athena++ at a pinned commit.

  • Shu–Osher: independent reference and mesh refinement

    Compare shock–density-wave interaction against separately executed Athena++ reference data rather than a finer grid of the same Python code.

  • GLM longitudinal mode: analytic damping

    Compare a longitudinal magnetic Fourier mode of GLM cleaning with the continuous linear matrix-exponential solution.

CSVR and Langevin: distributions, fluctuations and correlations

42 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Exact CSVR bath: conditional laws, fluctuations and kinetic correlation

    Implement the bath-only CSVR update for 1, 2 and 6 degrees of freedom; compare exact conditional moments/noncentral-chi-square laws, canonical Gamma fluctuations and kinetic correlations.

  • CSVR versus Langevin: distributions, fluctuations and correlations of two harmonic modes

    Compare global CSVR with local Langevin on two nondegenerate modes using the same OBABO splitting. Record position, momentum, temperature and total-energy distributions, fluctuations and correlations.

  • Anharmonic CSVR versus Langevin: distribution checks against Boltzmann quadrature

    Compare CSVR and Langevin distributions, temperature/energy fluctuations and correlations for two weakly quartic modes. Independent Boltzmann quadrature provides equilibrium references.

Independent validation of nonlinear reaction–diffusion and pattern formation

28 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Finite-amplitude Schnakenberg transient: nonlinearity and independent reference

    Validate a finite-amplitude reaction-diffusion transient to t=8 with an independent Fourier–Galerkin reference, grid/time convergence and newly generated harmonics.

  • Near-saturated Schnakenberg pattern: near-stationarity and grid/time convergence

    Compare the selected1D pattern at t=100 with an independent reference. Separate reference90→100 change/full-PDE residual from the finite-step primary FD residual.

Multimodal MCMC: independent checks of poor mixing and convergence diagnostics

39 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • Multimodal MCMC: trapping, false reassurance and a global proposal

    Compare dispersed local MH, same-mode local MH and symmetry-informed reflection/local MH on the same bimodal target. Separate detecting expected mixing failures from successful finite-budget mode exploration.

  • Independent MCMC diagnostic checks: rank Rhat, bulk/tail ESS and degeneracies

    Actually process62 byte-identical datasets with separately installed ArviZ0.20.0, bound by recipe/input/chain/official-source hashes. Separate finite-value agreement from explicit degeneracy-policy differences.

CT and GLM: matched controls, independent checks and grid/time convergence

46 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • CT and GLM: mesh/time convergence of a moving magnetostatic equilibrium

    Use a constructed exact equilibrium with nonzero magnetic force balanced by a pressure gradient, matching CT/GLM initial cell states, flux speed bound and time-step sequence.

  • Orszag–Tang: matched CT/GLM and converged independent references

    Run CT/GLM with the same OT initial cell averages and dt sequence, comparing with separately executed Athena++ references whose mesh/time sensitivities are separated.

  • Initial-constraint preservation by CT and active GLM cleaning: nonphysical control

    Intentionally begin with the same non-solenoidal cell B, checking that CT does not remove the initial defect and calibrating GLM’s B/psi response. This diagnostic adds no physical problem.

Numerical foundations: conditioning, residuals, convergence and independent references

49 tests passed; every input rerun from an independent ZIP extraction.

  • AMG: conditioning, anisotropy, residual and forward error

    Separate condition number from anisotropy on SPD systems of 49, 225 and 961 unknowns. Compare analytic spectra, independent sparse direct solutions and DST diagonalization, and measure how residual translates to forward error.

  • QR/SVD: prescribed spectra, perturbation gain and numerical rank

    Vary condition number from 1 to 2³⁶ for exactly representable 16×4 dyadic matrices, separating reconstruction from forward solution error. Compare LAPACK and separately executed 80/120-digit references for the actual binary64 inputs.

  • Krylov eigenvalues: cluster subspaces and nonnormal sensitivity

    Measure Lanczos and Arnoldi with dimensions4/8/16/32 on 48-dimensional clustered symmetric and strongly nonnormal triangular matrices. Distinguish residual, forward error, eigenvalue condition number and invariant subspace.

  • Quadrature: narrow peaks, cancellation, endpoints and estimator blind spots

    Vary Gauss order and adaptive tolerance on narrow Lorentz peaks, a square-root endpoint, severe cancellation, a zero integral and a deliberately missed compact bump, using analytic integrals and QUADPACK.
