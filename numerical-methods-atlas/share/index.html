固定入力から実行できる22例を選ぶ

各リンクで条件、版、入力、実行手順、JSON/CSVと許容誤差を確認できます。

  • Barnes–Hut：192粒子と直接和の比較192粒子の木近似と直接和を比べ、力の誤差・全開極限・相互作用回数を確認する。
  • 水素型原子：解析解と格子収束Z=1,2の指定された束縛状態について、エネルギー・動径波動関数・格子収束を解析参照と比べる。
  • He：閉殻HF・交換のみLDA球対称1s²のHeをRHFと交換のみLDAで解き、SCF収束・エネルギー・電子数を独立な有限基底参照と照合する。
  • 周期正弦波の線形移流：風上法と解析解周期正弦波の輸送を解析的な平行移動と比べ、格子細分化・平均値保存・最大値原理を確認する。
  • Sod衝撃波管：Godunov型更新と自己相似解Sod衝撃波管を自己相似Riemann解と比べ、密度誤差の減少・正値性・境界流束を含む収支を確認する。
  • Shu–Osher：特性WENO-Zと高解像度参照衝撃波と密度波の相互作用を計算し、100/200点解と800点の同一手法による数値参照との差を確認する。
  • 1次元Poisson：Dirichlet正弦解と2次収束Thomas法の数値解をsin(πx)と比べ、連続解誤差・独立な離散残差・2次格子収束を分けて確認する。
  • 2次元Poisson：CG・多重格子とP1 FEMCG・多重格子の同じ差分解とP1有限要素を調べ、FD最大誤差とFEMのL2・H1半ノルム収束を見比べる。
  • 1次元Bratu：小さい枝のNewton–GMRES小さい枝をNewton–GMRESで求め、解析解・独立な非線形残差・2次格子収束を確認する。
  • 調和振動子：Verletの位相誤差とエネルギーVerlet軌道を解析的な振動と比べ、状態誤差・元のエネルギーの偏差・時間反転を確認する。
  • 硬い余弦追従ODE：SDIRK2硬い余弦追従問題の解析解と刻み細分化を比べ、別の強減衰問題でL安定な応答を確認する。
  • 滑らかな関数：Legendre射影とGLL補間exp(x)のLegendre射影とGLL補間を比較し、次数による誤差減少・係数・直交性を別の高次求積で確認する。
  • 周期PM粒子群：質量・内力・格子場固定19粒子のCIC質量・自己力・内力和・周期折返しを調べ、別の正弦密度で格子ポテンシャルと加速度を解析検証する。
  • 周期Taylor–Green渦：速度・散逸・発散周期渦の速度と粘性エネルギー減衰を解析解と比べ、離散発散・圧力ゲージ・格子と時間刻みへの依存を確認する。
  • Brio–Wu：HLLDと保存的に粗視化した数値参照HLLDの64/128セル解を512セル数値参照と比べ、密度・圧力・一定縦磁場・流束収支と切替回数を確認する。
  • ロジスティック増殖：解析解と刻み幅の収束無次元の単一個体群。RK4を全出力時刻で解析解と比較し、非負性と刻み幅の収束を確認。反応拡散・複数種は対象外。
  • SIR：独立DOP853参照・人口保存・収束閉鎖・均質な人口割合SIR。RK4と別実装のSciPy DOP853を比較。人口保存・非負性・時間収束を確認。SEIR・空間伝播・実データ較正は対象外。
  • Monte Carlo：256 seedの積分誤差と区間被覆U[0,1]でx²を積分。256の独立seedで解析積分・分散と比較し、Nの平方根に反比例するRMS誤差と区間被覆を確認。MCMC・希少事象は対象外。
  • Itô SDE：共有Brown運動による強収束スカラー幾何Brown運動のEuler–Maruyama。粗細刻みと解析解で同じBrown運動を共有し、4096経路で強RMS誤差を測定。一般SDE・MCMC・弱収束次数は検証しない。
  • H₂分子RHF：独立参照・SCF・s型基底拡大孤立・Born–Oppenheimer・非相対論・2電子閉殻H₂。3核間距離でSTO-3Gと入れ子s型Gaussian基底を比較。RHFの数値検証であり、分子DFT・周期系・完全基底極限や正しい解離を検証しない。
  • 周期HeのKS-LDA：Γ点・kメッシュ・基底・SCF固定した1原子単純立方Heセル（格子定数4 bohr、価電子2、非偏極LDA_X＋VWN5、局所GTH）。PySCFを使う実行・検証ワークフロー。11設定でk点・Gaussian軌道基底・FFT補助格子・SCFを別々に比較し、Γ／2³は独立DFTK平面波参照と照合。平衡He結晶・完全基底・金属・一般物質は未検証。
  • 周期He₂の原子間力：解析力・有限差分・BOMD接続非対称な2原子He周期セル・局所GTH・LDA交換＋VWN5。PySCFの解析力（Gaussian基底の微分を含む）を全6座標のエネルギー有限差分とDFTK平面波参照で照合し、力の基底・k点・SCF・FFT収束を分離。BOMDの力供給と短い刻み比較に限定し、長時間NVE・CPMD・一般物質は未検証。

  • Barnes–Hut: 192 particles against direct summationCompare tree and direct forces for 192 particles; inspect force error, the fully opened limit and interaction counts.
  • Hydrogenic atoms: analytic references and grid refinementCompare energy, radial wavefunctions and grid refinement with analytic references for the specified Z=1,2 bound states.
  • Helium: closed-shell HF and exchange-only LDASolve spherical 1s² helium with RHF and exchange-only LDA; compare SCF convergence, energies and electron count with independent finite-basis references.
  • Periodic sine advection: upwind and analytic transportCompare periodic sine transport with analytic translation; check refinement, mean conservation and the maximum principle.
  • Sod shock tube: Godunov-type update and self-similar referenceCompare the Sod shock tube with a self-similar Riemann solution; check decreasing density error, positivity and boundary-flux balances.
  • Shu–Osher: characteristic WENO-Z and a numerical referenceCompute shock–density-wave interaction and compare 100/200-point solutions with an 800-point same-method numerical reference.
  • 1D Poisson: Dirichlet sine solution and second-order refinementCompare the Thomas solution with sin(πx), separating continuous error, a recomputed discrete residual and second-order grid refinement.
  • 2D Poisson: CG, multigrid and P1 FEMInspect CG/multigrid on the same discrete system and P1 FEM, comparing FD maximum error with separate FEM L2/H1-seminorm refinement.
  • 1D Bratu: Newton–GMRES on the lower branchCompute the lower branch with Newton–GMRES and check its analytic solution, recomputed nonlinear residual and second-order refinement.
  • Harmonic oscillator: Verlet phase error and energyCompare Verlet trajectories with analytic oscillation; inspect state error, original-energy deviation and time reversal.
  • Stiff cosine-tracking ODE: SDIRK2Compare stiff cosine tracking with its analytic solution under step refinement, plus a separate strongly damped problem.
  • Smooth function: Legendre projection and GLL interpolationCompare Legendre projection and GLL interpolation of exp(x); use higher-order reference quadrature for p-refinement, coefficients and orthogonality.
  • Periodic PM particles: mass, internal force and mesh fieldInspect CIC mass, self-force, net internal force and wrapping for 19 fixed particles; separately check mesh potential/acceleration for a sinusoidal density.
  • Periodic Taylor–Green vortex: velocity, decay and divergenceCompare periodic-vortex velocity and viscous energy decay with analytic references; inspect discrete divergence, pressure gauge and space/time sensitivity.
  • Brio–Wu: HLLD and a conservatively restricted numerical referenceCompare 64/128-cell HLLD solutions with a 512-cell numerical reference; inspect density, pressure, constant longitudinal field, balances and fallbacks.
  • Logistic growth: analytic solution and time refinementOne dimensionless population. Compare RK4 with the analytic solution at every output time; check nonnegativity and time refinement. Excludes reaction–diffusion and multispecies models.
  • SIR: independent DOP853 reference, conservation and convergenceClosed homogeneous population-fraction SIR. Compare RK4 with independently implemented SciPy DOP853; check conservation, nonnegativity and time convergence. Excludes SEIR, spatial spread and calibration to observations.
  • Monte Carlo: integral errors and interval coverage across 256 seedsIntegrate x² under U[0,1]. Compare 256 independent seeds with analytic integral and variance, checking N^-1/2 RMS scaling and interval coverage. Excludes MCMC and rare events.
  • Itô SDE: strong convergence with coupled Brownian pathsEuler–Maruyama for scalar geometric Brownian motion. Coarse/fine steps and analytic endpoints share the same Brownian paths; measure strong RMS error across 4096 paths. Does not validate general SDEs, MCMC or weak convergence order.
  • H₂ molecular RHF: independent reference, SCF and s-basis refinementIsolated Born–Oppenheimer nonrelativistic two-electron closed-shell H₂. Compare STO-3G and nested s-Gaussian spaces at three separations. Numerical RHF validation, not molecular DFT, periodic, complete-basis or correct-dissociation validation.
  • Periodic-He KS-LDA: Gamma, k meshes, basis and SCFFixed one-atom simple-cubic He cell (lattice constant 4 bohr, two valence electrons, unpolarized LDA_X plus VWN5, local GTH). A PySCF execution/validation workflow. Eleven settings separately compare k sampling, Gaussian orbital basis, auxiliary FFT grid and SCF; Gamma and 2³ are compared with independent DFTK plane waves. Equilibrium helium, complete-basis, metals and general materials remain unvalidated.
  • Periodic-He₂ forces: analytic derivatives, finite differences and BOMD interfaceAsymmetric two-He periodic cell with local GTH and LDA exchange plus VWN5. Compare PySCF analytic forces, including Gaussian-basis derivatives, with energy finite differences for all six coordinates and independent DFTK plane waves. Separate force basis, k-point, SCF and FFT refinement. BOMD coverage is a force interface and short step comparison; long NVE, CPMD and general materials remain unvalidated.
