A numerical-computation resource for graduate students, researchers and the AI assistants supporting their research and implementation. Find methods through words, formulas or pictures; examine assumptions and original papers; then follow fixed inputs and public code to reference values and tolerances for 20 representative problems. Analytic solutions, independent references and higher-resolution numerical comparisons are distinguished, alongside scoped educational examples, reading-only material and planned coverage.
