04 / READING THE RECORDS

Context travels with every number.

One candidate, a result history

Submitted manuscripts, revised papers, conference talks and official updates remain separate evidence stages. A later score does not erase the earlier paper result. Revision, measurement, presentation and checking dates are distinct.

Unknown is a useful result

A machine’s advertised capacity is not an application result. Missing precision and missing throughput are labelled explicitly. These records are not a performance ranking.

Sources at the field level

Selection, award result, bibliography, abstract and measurements link to their own evidence. English summaries are editorial; original abstracts remain available at the source.
